p(x) = x^2 - 2x on X = [0, 2], whose range is [-1, 0]:
  x*x - 2*x              [-4, 4]
  x*(x - 2)              [-4, 0]
  (x - 1)*(x - 1) - 1    [-2, 0]
The hull over n pieces of X:
  n      x*x - 2*x                      (x - 1)*(x - 1) - 1
  1      [-4, 4]                        [-2, 0]
  2      [-3, 2]                        [-1, 0]
  4      [-2, 1]                        [-1, 0]
  16     [-1.25, 0.25]                  [-1, 0]
  64     [-1.0625, 0.0625]              [-1, 0]
  256    [-1.015625, 0.015625]          [-1, 0]
  1024   [-1.00390625, 0.00390625]      [-1, 0]

  x*x - 2*x              [-6, 4]
  (x - 1)*(x - 1) - 1    [-2, 2.220446049e-15]
